    Our Programs

    We run four core programs designed to uplift and empower children in our communities.

    Education Support

    Providing school supplies, tuition assistance, and tutoring to help children stay in school and excel.

    SRHR Mentorship

    Age-appropriate sexual and reproductive health education through trusted mentors and safe spaces.

    Life Skills

    Teaching practical life skills including financial literacy, communication, and leadership.

    Child Mentorship

    One-on-one mentoring that builds confidence, resilience, and a positive vision for the future.
