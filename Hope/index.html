ABOUT US

Building Hope, One Child at a Time.

At Hope in a Bag, we believe that every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow and eat. Our mission is to provide orphans and vulnerable children across Kenya with the educational tools they need to build a brighter future.

Through generous donations, we supply school bags filled with essential items including notebooks, pens, pencils, textbooks and other learning materials.

Education is the key to breaking the cycle of poverty, and together we can ensure that no child is left behind.

Compassion

We lead with love and empathy in every interaction with the children we serve.

Mission

To empower vulnerable children and adolescents through the provision of educational materials, nutritional support, and reproductive health resources.

Vision

A community where every child and youth has access to basic needs, quality education, and comprehensive health information to reach their full potential.

Integrity

Transparency and accountability guide everything we do.
